Privacy Policy

Who Did Us (whodidus.com) is a small, read-mostly website. This policy says what information the site collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • If you use the Suggest a Case box, we receive what you type into it. Please don't send personal information about yourself or about private individuals.
  • Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may record standard technical information — pages visited, browser type, and approximate location from your IP address — to keep the site running and see which cases people read.

What we do with it

  • Case suggestions are read by the site's editors and used only to research possible cases.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not rent it, trade it, or share it for marketing.
  • We do not run user accounts, and we never ask for passwords, payment information, or government ID numbers.

Cookies

  • The site and its embedded video and analytics providers may set cookies. You can block cookies in your browser; the site will still work.

Links to other sites

  • Case pages link to public documents on government and other third-party sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Contact

  • Questions about this policy: use the Suggest a Case page and say it's about privacy.
