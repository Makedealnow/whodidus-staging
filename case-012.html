Case 012

$3.9M for LGBTQI+ in the Western Balkans

The chart said $3.9 million — and for once, the record agrees to the dollar. The full program was priced at $6.5 million.

This case is on Senator Kennedy's July 15 list — watch him read it on the Senate floor.

  • The real program: SHIELD — a USAID cooperative agreement with the American Bar Association, signed September 30, 2024 — total obligations $3,929,783, the chart figure to the dollar
  • A five-year, $6.5 million program across Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, North Macedonia, and Serbia — building up LGBTQI+ organizations and mapping anti-rights disinformation networks
  • Stop-worked in January 2025 and terminated by USAID on February 24, 2025 — only $704,160 was ever paid out

Who did us

The money (2024)

  • Joe Biden — President of the United States Signed the FY2024 funding law (P.L. 118-47) on March 23, 2024; its lump-sum State and Foreign Operations accounts are where the money came from. The House passed it 286–134 and the Senate 74–24 — and no vote ever named this program.

The program (2024)

  • Samantha Power — Administrator, USAID Ran the agency when the SHIELD agreement was signed on September 30, 2024.
  • Jay Gilliam — Senior LGBTQI+ Coordinator, USAID Gave the opening remarks at the January 23, 2024 conference for the LGBTQI+ funding competition — the solicitation that produced this award — presenting the agency's LGBTQI+ Inclusive Development Policy.
  • Bama Athreya — Deputy Assistant Administrator, Bureau for Inclusive Growth, Partnership, and Innovation, USAID Oversaw the Inclusive Development Hub in the bureau that funded the award, from January 2022 to January 2025.
  • William R. Bay — President, American Bar Association Became the ABA's president on August 6, 2024, and led the association when it took the award.
  • Scott Carlson — Associate Executive Director, ABA Center for Global Programs Ran the ABA center that implemented the program; his sworn February 11, 2025 court declaration lists "Western Balkans Equality SHIELD — $3,929,783" among the ABA's USAID awards.
The chart said $3.9 million. The record says $3,929,783 in obligations on one award — and no bill ever named this item: Congress voted lump-sum foreign-aid money, and USAID designed the competition and chose the recipient itself. One honest gap: no public document names the USAID officer who signed the September 30, 2024 agreement, so this page doesn't name one either.

Who's rescuing us

The 2025 exposure and the end

  • House Foreign Affairs Committee — Republican staff Published the award itself on February 13, 2025 — its exact total, $3,929,783, and its project number, 7200AA24CA00023.
  • John Kennedy — U.S. Senator, Louisiana Put "$3.9 million for LGBTQI+ populations in the Western Balkans" on his Senate floor chart and read it aloud on July 15, 2025, backing the rescissions package.
  • Donald Trump — President of the United States Sent Congress the 2025 rescissions package; his administration terminated the SHIELD program on February 24, 2025, five months into its five years.
