Case 011

$5.1M for Queer Global Movements

The chart said $5.1 million. The real program cost even more — $5,970,257 in obligations on one USAID agreement.

This case is on Senator Kennedy's July 15 list — watch him read it on the Senate floor.

  • The real program: the Alliance for Global Equality — a cooperative agreement USAID signed with Outright International on August 25, 2022; total obligations $5,970,257, of which $2,574,383 was paid out
  • It made grants to LGBTQI+-led organizations in countries around the world — 39 grants in 16 countries by March 2024 — with the LGBTQ+ Victory Institute as Outright's partner
  • The January 2025 foreign-aid freeze halted it, and in August 2025 the White House moved to rescind the Democracy Fund money behind it

Who did us

The money (2022)

  • Joe Biden — President of the United States Signed the FY2022 funding law (P.L. 117-103) on March 15, 2022; its lump-sum foreign-aid accounts — above all the Democracy Fund — are where the money came from.

The program (2022–2025)

  • Samantha Power — Administrator, USAID Ran the agency when the agreement was signed on August 25, 2022, and highlighted the Alliance herself in a January 2023 meeting readout.
  • Jay Gilliam — Senior LGBTQI+ Coordinator, USAID The agency's senior coordinator for these programs; he fronted USAID's support for the Alliance at its launch.
  • Maria Sjödin — Executive Director, Outright International Took the job on August 4, 2022 — three weeks before the signing — and ran the organization that received the award.
  • Annise Parker — President & CEO, LGBTQ+ Victory Institute Led the Victory Institute, Outright's co-lead partner in the Alliance.
The figure said $5.1 million. The record says $5,970,257 in obligations on one agreement — and no bill ever named this item: Congress voted lump-sum foreign-aid money, and USAID chose the program. One honest gap: no public document names the USAID officer who signed the August 25, 2022 agreement, so this page doesn't name one either.

Who's rescuing us

The 2025 figure and the end

  • Russell Vought — Director, Office of Management and Budget His budget office published the $5.1 million figure on June 3, 2025, in the rollout of the President's rescissions package. He is still the budget director today.
  • John Kennedy — U.S. Senator, Louisiana Read the $5.1 million figure aloud on the Senate floor on July 15, 2025, from a chart of taxpayer spending.
  • Jodey Arrington — Chairman, House Budget Committee Published the item on June 13, 2025, in his rescissions list.
  • Donald Trump — President of the United States Sent Congress the 2025 rescissions package; his administration froze the program's funding in January 2025, and his White House's August 2025 pocket rescission targeted the Democracy Fund money behind the Alliance, listing $4 million for it.
