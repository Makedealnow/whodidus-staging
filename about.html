About Who Did Us

Who Did Us is a public record project. Behind every outrageous case — a spending program, a payout, a deal the public paid for — there are real people: somebody voted for it, somebody signed it, somebody approved it, somebody ran it.

We find those people in the public documents and name them, with the documents linked on every case page so anyone can check our work.

It is not government-only. Any category counts, as long as the record names names.

If a document doesn't name you, we don't either.
