Disclaimer

Who Did Us reports what public documents say. A person's name appears in a case for one reason: a public document — a bill, a roll-call vote, a grant record, an agency document, or an audit — names that person in connection with the matter.

Being named here is not an accusation of a crime, corruption, or personal misconduct. Voting for a law, signing a document, approving a program, or running one are official acts, and we describe them in factual terms only.

Figures quoted in a case (amounts, dates, vote counts) come from the linked documents. Where a number circulated publicly but cannot be tied to a specific document, the case page says so.

If you believe a case misstates what a document says, send the document through the Suggest a Case page. If the document proves us wrong, we fix the case and post the fix on the case page.
