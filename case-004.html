Case 004

$3M for Sesame Street in Iraq

The chart said $3 million. The real program was $20 million on paper — $13 million of it actually obligated.

This case is on Senator Kennedy's July 15 list — watch him read it on the Senate floor.

  • The real program: "Ahlan Simsim," a USAID cooperative agreement with Sesame Workshop signed June 16, 2021 — a $20 million program on paper, $13,000,000 actually obligated, $5,910,150.39 paid out
  • The money came from the Economic Support Fund — one lump sum of $3,151,963,000 in the FY2021 foreign-aid law; the word "Sesame" appears in that law zero times
  • The program had been extended to run through June 30, 2027; it was terminated in the 2025 cuts, and Sesame Workshop closed its Iraq operations

Who did us

The money (2020)

  • Nita Lowey — Chair, House State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Chaired the House subcommittee that wrote the foreign-aid bill carrying the lump-sum Economic Support Fund.
  • Hal Rogers — Ranking Member, House State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Ranking member of the House subcommittee that wrote the foreign-aid bill.
  • Patrick Leahy — Ranking Member, Senate State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Ranking member of the Senate subcommittee that wrote the foreign-aid bill.

The program (2021–2024)

  • Samantha Power — Administrator, USAID Ran the agency when the agreement was signed on June 16, 2021, and through every funding increase that followed.
  • John Cardenas — Mission Director, USAID/Iraq Ran the Iraq mission that signed the cooperative agreement in June 2021.
  • Elise Jensen — Mission Director, USAID/Iraq Ran the Iraq mission during the later funding increases, in post by December 29, 2022.
  • Steve Youngwood — Chief Executive, Sesame Workshop Led the organization that received the award, from January 1, 2021 until February 2024.
  • Sherrie Westin — President, Sesame Workshop Became President on January 1, 2021, running the social-impact and philanthropy side of the organization — the side that holds programs like this one.
  • Rene Celaya — Managing Director for Ahlan Simsim, Sesame Workshop Led the Ahlan Simsim program at Sesame Workshop, documented in the role in 2022.
The funding law (P.L. 116-260) passed the House 359–53 on the division carrying foreign aid and the Senate 92–6, both on December 21, 2020, and was signed on December 27, 2020. It appropriated the Economic Support Fund as one lump sum — no project lines, and no mention of Sesame Street. One honest gap: no public document names the USAID officer who signed the agreement, so this page doesn't name one either.

Who's rescuing us

The 2025 figure

  • Russell Vought — Director, Office of Management and Budget His April 2025 memo flagged "$3 million for Iraqi Sesame Street" as an example of the waste that could continue at USAID without the rescission.
  • John Kennedy — U.S. Senator, Louisiana Put "$3,000,000: Sesame Street in Iraq" on his Senate floor chart during the 2025 rescissions debate.
  • Jodey Arrington — Chairman, House Budget Committee Published the same "$3 million for Iraqi Sesame Street" item on his June 13, 2025 rescissions list.
  • Donald Trump — President of the United States Sent Congress the 2025 rescissions package, and his administration terminated this award — it is on the termination list dated March 1, 2025.
