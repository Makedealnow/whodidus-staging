Case 001

$6M for Palestinian Media

A 2025 budget memo put this on a waste list. No member of Congress ever voted on it — it was never in a bill.

This case is on Senator Kennedy's July 15 list — watch him read it on the Senate floor.

  • The only matching program in the record: USAID's "Enhancing Palestinian Independent Media," 2010–2014, run by Internews — total cost $5,999,995
  • The money came from a lump-sum foreign aid fund Congress passed in 2009; USAID officials chose this program themselves
  • None of the USAID officials behind it still works for the government

The names

The 2025 figure

  • Russell Vought — Director, Office of Management and Budget Wrote the 2025 memo that put "$6 million for Palestinian media and civic society support" on a waste list. He is still the budget director today.
  • John Kennedy — U.S. Senator, Louisiana Read the figure aloud on the Senate floor on July 15, 2025, from a chart of taxpayer spending.
  • Jodey Arrington — Chairman, House Budget Committee Published the same example list in June 2025.
  • Donald Trump — President of the United States Sent Congress the 2025 rescission package. The formal message contained no Palestinian-media item.

The money (2009)

  • Barack Obama — President of the United States Signed the FY2010 funding law on December 16, 2009; its lump-sum aid fund is where the money came from.
  • Nita Lowey — Chair, House State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Wrote the foreign-aid bill that carried the money.
  • Patrick Leahy — Chair, Senate State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Wrote the Senate version of the foreign-aid bill.
  • David Obey — Chairman, House Appropriations Committee Led the committee that produced the funding law.
  • Daniel Inouye — Chairman, Senate Appropriations Committee Led the Senate committee that produced the funding law.

The program (2010–2014)

  • Rajiv Shah — Administrator, USAID Ran the agency that made the award. He now runs the Rockefeller Foundation.
  • Howard Sumka — Mission Director, USAID West Bank & Gaza In charge when the solicitation went out in May 2010. He retired from USAID that year.
  • Roy Plucknett — Agreement Officer, USAID Signed the solicitation that offered the money. He died in 2022.
  • Michael T. Harvey — Mission Director, USAID West Bank & Gaza In charge when the award began in September 2010. He has since retired from USAID.
  • Dave Harden — Mission Director, USAID West Bank & Gaza Ran the mission during the program's final year. He now runs a private consulting firm, the Georgetown Strategy Group.
  • David Hoffman — Chief Executive, Internews Network Led the organization that received the award in 2010.
  • Jeanne Bourgault — President, Internews Network Ran Internews day to day for most of the program, from April 2011.
278 members of Congress voted yes on the 2009 funding law (221 in the House, 57 in the Senate). 70 of them are still in Congress today.
