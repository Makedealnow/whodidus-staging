Case 009

$833K for Key Populations in Nepal

The chart said $833,000. The real awards totaled $2.5 million — three times the chart figure.

This case is on Senator Kennedy's July 15 list — watch him read it on the Senate floor.

  • The real program: USAID Sustainable HIV Epidemic Control — two awards signed in September 2024 by USAID/Nepal, $2,500,000 obligated combined ($5,000,000 total estimated cost)
  • AMDA-Nepal received $300,000 obligated; the Blue Diamond Society received $2,200,000 obligated — for HIV prevention, care, and treatment for key populations in Nepal
  • Both awards were terminated in February 2025

Who did us

The money (2024)

  • Chris Coons — Chair, Senate State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Chaired the Senate subcommittee that wrote the FY2024 foreign operations bill carrying the lump-sum Global Health Programs account.
  • Mario Díaz-Balart — Chair, House State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Chaired the House subcommittee that wrote the FY2024 foreign operations bill.
  • Barbara Lee — Ranking Member, House State & Foreign Operations Subcommittee Ranking member of the House subcommittee that wrote the FY2024 foreign operations bill.

The program (2024)

  • Samantha Power — Administrator, USAID Ran the agency when both awards were signed in September 2024.
  • Katie Donohoe — Mission Director, USAID/Nepal Headed the mission that issued both awards in September 2024.
  • Manisha Dhakal — Executive Director, Blue Diamond Society Led the organization that received the $2,200,000 award (72036724FA00004).
  • Dr. Pashupati Regmi — Chairperson, AMDA-Nepal Chaired the organization that received the $300,000 award (72036724FA00005).
No member of Congress ever voted on these awards as items — they appear in no bill text. The money came from the lump-sum Global Health Programs account in the FY2024 law (P.L. 118-47, Division F), and no public document names the USAID officer who signed the awards.

Who's rescuing us

  • Russell Vought — Director, Office of Management and Budget His office's June 3, 2025 rescissions rollout flagged the $833,000 Nepal item, and he listed it again in his written Senate testimony.
  • John Kennedy — U.S. Senator, Louisiana Read the item on the Senate floor on June 24, 2025, and again on July 15, 2025, from his chart of taxpayer spending.
  • Jodey Arrington — Chairman, House Budget Committee Published the same example list on June 13, 2025, including the $833,000 Nepal item.
  • Donald Trump — President of the United States Sent Congress the rescissions package on June 3, 2025; his administration terminated both Nepal awards in February 2025.
