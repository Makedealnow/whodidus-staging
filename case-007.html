Case 007

$67K for Insect Powder in Madagascar

The chart said $67,000. The real grant was $200,000 — three times more.

Senator Kennedy read this item on the Senate floor on June 24, 2025, and again on July 9, 2025.

  • The real program: a USAID Development Innovation Ventures Stage 1 grant (award 7200AA23FA00011) to Valala Farm of Antananarivo, signed August 23, 2023 by USAID headquarters — $200,000
  • It tested a cricket-powder-fortified instant porridge and crispbread for primary school children
  • USAID paid out $152,500; $47,500 was never paid, and the grant landed on DOGE's termination list dated March 1, 2025

Who did us

The program (2023)

  • Samantha Power — Administrator, USAID Ran the agency that made the award; she was in office when the grant was signed on August 23, 2023.
  • Sasha Gallant — Chief, Development Innovation Ventures (DIV), USAID Ran the headquarters office that selected and made this grant in 2023.
  • Brian Fisher — Founder, Valala Farm Founded the organization that received the grant.
The grant was signed by USAID headquarters (M/OAA), not by the Madagascar mission — and no public document names the individual officer who signed it, so this page doesn't name one either. The money came from the lump-sum Development Assistance account in the FY2023 funding law (P.L. 117-328); no bill ever named this project.

Who's rescuing us

The 2025 figure and the end

  • Russell Vought — Director, Office of Management and Budget His office's June 2025 rescissions rollout flagged "$67,000 for testing insect powder nutrition on children in Madagascar." He is still the budget director today.
  • John Kennedy — U.S. Senator, Louisiana Read the figure on the Senate floor on June 24, 2025, wrote it in a New York Post column on July 8, 2025, and read it on the floor again on July 9, 2025.
  • Jodey Arrington — Chairman, House Budget Committee Published the same figure in his rescissions list on June 13, 2025.
  • Donald Trump — President of the United States Sent Congress the 2025 rescissions package, and his administration terminated this grant — it is on DOGE's termination list dated March 1, 2025.
